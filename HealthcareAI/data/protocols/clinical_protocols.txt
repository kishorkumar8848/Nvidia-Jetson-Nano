Clinical Protocol for Fever and Headache:
For any patient (including infants, children, or adults) presenting with fever, high temperature, or headache:
1. Apply cold compress to the forehead to bring down temperature.
2. Administer Paracetamol 500mg tablet twice daily (reduce dosage for children as per age weight chart).
3. Keep the patient hydrated with plenty of water or oral rehydration solution.

Clinical Protocol for Cold and Cough:
For patients presenting with common cold, runny nose, or cough:
1. Advise warm saline water gargles three times daily to soothe the throat.
2. Give warm water for drinking and recommend steam inhalation twice daily.
3. Prescribe standard cough syrup and refer to a doctor if symptoms persist for more than 5 days.

Clinical Protocol for Skin Rash:
For any baby, child, or patient presenting with high fever and skin rash:
1. Refer immediately to the primary health center if a severe rash, stiff neck, or breathing difficulty is present.
2. If the rash is mild and there are no other danger signs, administer Paracetamol to manage fever and keep skin clean and dry.
